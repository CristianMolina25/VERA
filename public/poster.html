01 Abstract & Motivation

Digital fraud and psychological manipulation—such as Phishing, Smishing (SMS deception), and Quishing (QR code obfuscation)—represent over 80% of personal data breaches. Existing solutions act as opaque enterprise firewalls that fail to empower everyday citizens.

VERA bridges this vulnerability by unifying deterministic client heuristics, real-time threat intelligence (Safe Browsing), and guardrailed Multimodal AI into a reliable, privacy-first risk evaluator.

02 Objectives & Design Pillars
  • Tri-Verdict Risk Classifier: Clear categorization: Probably Safe, Suspicious, or Dangerous.
  • Zero-Knowledge Privacy: Scrub credit cards, national IDs, and credentials locally before external processing.
  • High-Resilience Fallback: Maintain deterministic heuristics if AI APIs face outages or rate quotas.
  • Actionable Plain Language: Replace obscure cryptographic warnings with clear protective protocols.
03 Privacy Sanitization Engine

Sensitive data is redacted using deterministic regular expressions prior to server transmission:

Card Sanitizer: \b(?:\d[ -]?){13,19}\b → [CARD REDACTED]
ID & Phone: \b\d{7,12}\b → [NUMBER REDACTED]
Email Shield: [\w.+-]+@... → [EMAIL REDACTED]
⚡ The 5-Stage Step-by-Step Pipeline

The sequential architecture executing on each analyzed message, link, or image:

1

Multi-Modal Ingestion & Edge QR Decoding

User inputs text, SMS, link, or uploads an image. In-browser jsQR decodes embedded QR targets in memory, preventing unauthorized image leaks to remote servers.

2

Deterministic Privacy Scrubbing

The redactSensitiveData filter strips credit cards, passwords, and Colombian national IDs. Only contextual semantic tokens are passed downstream.

3

Heuristic Domain & Urgency Analysis

Parses canonical hostnames against verified institutional whitelist (whatsapp.com, bancolombia.com, gov.co). Flags typosquatting and psychological pressure keywords (urgente, suspenderán).

4

Threat Intelligence & AI Consensus

Paralleled queries to Google Safe Browsing v4 and VirusTotal v3 combined with structured Gemini / GPT-4o multimodal prompts enforcing deterministic JSON schemas.

5

Verdict Synthesis & Defensive Action Protocol

The Synthesizer reconciles discrepancies, enforces guardrails against false positives, and renders the user's color verdict and actionable security recommendations.
